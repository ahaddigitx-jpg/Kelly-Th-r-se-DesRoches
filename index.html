04 / About

Keep the warmth. Sharpen the through-line.

Your current About section is personable and detailed. It already establishes your experience and values. The revised version leads with the shared thread across your roles, then gives each role a clear place in the story.

Current About, excerpt

“At the core of everything I do is a commitment to creating meaningful connections, opening doors, elevating voices, and building platforms that bring people and communities together.”

What to improve

Bring this strong idea to the opening, reduce repeated descriptions of connection, and end with a simple invitation to connect with professionals and community leaders across South Florida.

Proposed About section

I believe the right connection can open a door, strengthen a business, or bring a community closer together. That idea sits at the center of my work across private-club membership, mortgage lending, media, and community engagement. As Executive Director of Membership at Tower Club Fort Lauderdale, I connect professionals, entrepreneurs, business leaders, and civic voices with a community built around relationships, shared experiences, and opportunities to grow. My work spans membership development, personalized outreach, member engagement, strategic partnerships, and events that bring South Florida’s business community together. I also work as a Mortgage Loan Originator with Starnet Lending, helping clients navigate the home-financing process and explore lending solutions aligned with their goals. Across both roles, I value listening first, understanding what matters to each person, and building relationships that last beyond a single conversation. Media is another way I help people and ideas reach a wider audience. Through hosting and producing Tower Talks, I spotlight the entrepreneurs, business leaders, public servants, and community organizations shaping Fort Lauderdale. I’m drawn to stories that reveal the people behind progress and create more ways for others to connect. My professional background includes finance, business development, private clubs, communications, and community-focused initiatives. I’m especially interested in the intersection of relationships, leadership, and local impact. If you’re a professional, entrepreneur, or community leader looking to build meaningful connections across South Florida, I’d be glad to connect. Let’s start a conversation.

Review details for accuracy and personal voice before publishing.
